# slingshot-marketing

Public marketing site for Slingshot IEP (www.slingshotiep.com). Next.js App Router.

## Referral QR codes (read before touching flyers, partners or /go links)

Every referring organization gets its own random referral code, its own
English and Spanish QR codes, and a landing page that says "Shared by
<partner>". Never reuse another partner's QR code and never point a QR code
straight at the app. Use `node scripts/new-referral.mjs "<Partner name>"` and
follow `referrals/README.md`.

## Writing rules

- No em dashes, en dashes or double hyphens in any user-facing string.
- Person-first language: "children with autism", never "autistic children".
- Never call Slingshot "HIPAA compliant".
- English and Spanish pages say the same thing; write the Spanish naturally,
  not word for word.
